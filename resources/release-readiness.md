# Release-readiness checklist

PRI starter resource · v0.2.0 · October 17, 2026

Status: adaptable discussion template. Completing it is not a safety certification or a guarantee against incidents. The responsible organization defines applicable requirements and approval authority.

## Measurement and validation

- [ ] Journey boundaries and representative workloads are defined.
- [ ] Baseline and candidate conditions are comparable, or differences are documented.
- [ ] Completion, failed/incomplete outcomes, retries, and latency are considered.
- [ ] Noise, uncertainty, missing data, and sample limitations are documented.
- [ ] Applicable thresholds were agreed and have a stated rationale.
- [ ] Required regression checks have evidence, not only a pass/fail assertion.
- [ ] Relevant incidents and prior preventive actions were reviewed.
- [ ] Resource/cost impacts are considered where measurable and relevant.

## Risk and response

- [ ] Identified risks have owners and evidence-based dispositions.
- [ ] Threshold breaches or evidence gaps have mitigation and further-validation plans.
- [ ] Rollout, monitoring, rollback, and escalation arrangements are defined as applicable.
- [ ] Exceptions identify an authorized approver, rationale, supporting evidence, expiry, and conditions.
- [ ] The decision and outstanding actions are recorded.
