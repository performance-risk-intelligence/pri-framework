# Reviewing and adapting PRI materials

Public discussion guide · v0.4.0 · October 17, 2026

## Choose the right starting point

Start with the [overview](pri-overview.md), then the [assessment brief](readiness-assessment.md) for one bounded journey. Use the [public roadmap](public-roadmap.md) to distinguish current materials from planned development. The [worked example](worked-example.md) illustrates the connected records with invented data.

These instructions describe technical review and adaptation steps. They do not change the repository's licensing or grant additional reuse rights. Check applicable permissions before redistribution or adaptation.

## Adapt the method to a real workflow

- Record the source resource, edition, review date, and any local modifications. Keep an unmodified reference copy.
- Define the journey, owner, environments, authorized inputs, and intended decision before collecting evidence.
- Replace illustrative identifiers and thresholds with locally justified definitions. Preserve failure counts, missing data, uncertainty, and comparison limitations.
- Link each identified risk to evidence, an owner, a proposed control, a decision record, and follow-up verification.
- Review recommendations with the organization responsible for the system. That organization retains release authority and decides what to implement.
- Evaluate control maintenance and outcomes separately. A completed template or download does not demonstrate adoption or improved performance.

## Prepare an organizational discussion

Use this short outline through your existing communication channel. This page does not submit information or create an engagement.

- Materials reviewed: titles, versions, and actual review date.
- Proposed journey: start, usable completion, and operational question.
- Relevant evidence categories: benchmarks, change records, incident summaries, and telemetry where authorized; identify gaps without sharing restricted records.
- Practical fit: expected deliverables, responsible participants, access constraints, and implementation dependencies.
- Next decision: clarify questions, consider scope, defer pending prerequisites, or decline. Interest and a signed engagement are different states.

Commercial assessments and implementation remain planned for the roadmap's dissemination stage following framework development, subject to readiness and separate agreement.

## Prepare a technical review

A reviewer can identify their relevant expertise and relationship to the work, which materials they examined, and which statements they can independently support. Useful feedback distinguishes observed artifact features from expected benefits and identifies limitations, alternative approaches, and validation needed.

- Does the measurement procedure preserve comparable conditions and unsuccessful outcomes?
- Can a reader trace a signal through risk ownership, decision authority, and a preventive action?
- Which parts are established engineering practice, and is the proposed integration practically useful?
- What data and evaluation would be needed before relying on predictive scoring?
- Under what conditions could the materials be maintained by other teams, and what evidence would demonstrate that use?

A review of public materials is not evidence that the reviewer observed a deployment, customer result, or past achievement. Describe the actual basis of knowledge and review date. Avoid attributing statements or endorsements without permission.

## Keep review records proportionate and private

Share only authorized information through an appropriate existing channel. Do not put credentials, personal records, confidential telemetry, customer identities, or restricted technical documents in public repository discussions. General feedback can reference the existing [project repository](https://github.com/performance-risk-intelligence/pri-framework) and a specific file or commit without disclosing underlying private evidence.

Any later public account of feedback or use should have permission, a date, scope, and supporting records. Public progress updates should distinguish a discussion, proposed engagement, implemented control, and verified outcome.
