    Inspect economic inputs and calculations

    All values below are editable planning assumptions for the simulation, separate from PRI's business plan. Labor and cost savings are not measured by the event engine.

    Other fixed assumptions: 52 releases/year; three preflights per release; four incident-response person-hours without this preflight versus one with it, excluding routine release review; $120 annual noncompute overhead; $0.05 per active vCPU-hour as a hypothetical metered-work valuation.     What a real pilot must establish

    Define comparable journeys and exposure; retain baseline and candidate measurements; verify actual control execution; compare workload, staffing, pricing and architecture changes; and collect setup and maintenance effort. Report invoice effects and redeployable engineer-hours separately. A real control needs an owner and an effectiveness review over known exposure.
